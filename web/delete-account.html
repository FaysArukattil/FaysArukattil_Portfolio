Delete Your Buddy Account & Data

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data for Buddy: Auto Expense Tracker (package: com.faysarukattil.buddy), developed by Fays Arukattil.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Buddy app and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings (or Profile).
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and data are deleted right away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email faysarukattil@gmail.com with the subject "Buddy Account Deletion Request". Please send it from the email address linked to your Buddy account. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What data is deleted

  • Your account information (email address, name if provided, and user ID)
  • Your expense and transaction records, budgets and goals stored with your account
  • Your app preferences and settings stored with your account

What data is kept

We do not keep any of your personal data after deletion. Backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.

Guest users

If you use Buddy as a guest, your data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

Questions? Contact faysarukattil@gmail.com.
